Rise Robotics is a student-led initiative bringing practical STEM and robotics learning to students in Kisii, Kenya, in partnership with the GRACE Academic Center.

Support the Mission What We Do

Our Mission

Many students around the world lack access to hands-on STEM resources. Rise Robotics works to close that gap by providing structured, build-focused robotics education that encourages curiosity, problem-solving, and leadership.

What We Do

Robotics Foundations

Students learn core concepts in mechanics, motion, and design through hands-on robot builds and challenges.

Electronics & Sensors

Introduction to circuits, sensors, and how hardware interacts with the real world.

Programming Logic

Foundational programming concepts focused on reasoning, debugging, and controlling robotic systems.

Student Mentorship

A student-led model that emphasizes leadership, collaboration, and peer-to-peer learning.

Community Impact

By working directly with the GRACE Academic Center in Kisii, Kenya, Rise Robotics ensures that programs are locally grounded, sustainable, and aligned with student needs.

Support Rise Robotics

Donations help fund robotics components, learning materials, and consistent instruction—allowing students to keep building and learning.
