What Students Learn

Structured, build-first lessons designed to create real understanding and confidence.

Robotics foundations

Mechanisms, motion, stability, and design thinking through hands-on builds.

  • How movement works (gears, linkages, torque)
  • Iterative design: test → fix → improve
  • Working in teams

Electronics & sensors

Safe wiring, simple circuits, and sensing the environment.

  • Circuits and power basics
  • Sensors (distance, light, touch)
  • Cause-and-effect control

Programming logic

Foundational coding habits focused on reasoning and debugging.

  • Step-by-step thinking
  • Debugging like an engineer
  • Simple robot behaviors

Mentorship & leadership

A student-led model that builds confidence, communication, and leadership.

  • Peer-to-peer instruction
  • Presenting solutions
  • Helping others learn
