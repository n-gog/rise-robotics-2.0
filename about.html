About Rise Robotics

A student-led initiative focused on access, hands-on learning, and real STEM confidence.

What we believe

Robotics is one of the best “gateway” tools for STEM: it’s visual, collaborative, and teaches problem-solving through real builds. Students learn engineering habits—testing, debugging, iteration—not just theory.

Our mission is to expand access to that kind of learning for students in Kisii, Kenya, through a partnership with the GRACE Academic Center.

How we operate

  • Build-first sessions with clear objectives
  • Student-led mentorship and leadership
  • Progression from fundamentals → projects
  • Focus on sustainability and community partnership

Why “Rise”?

Because access to STEM shouldn’t be limited by geography or resources. Students rise when given tools and support.
